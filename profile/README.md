**Rakugaki – Doodle Lives** is a YouTube channel of short science videos. Each one takes a single question, such as
what would happen if the Sun disappeared, and works through it: what is really going on, the surprising part, and what
it means for you. *Rakugaki* is Japanese for "doodle".

### How the videos are made

- **Real sources.** Each script is written and fact-checked with AI from public sources such as Wikipedia and
  science paper summaries. Where scientists aren't sure, the video says so. Sources are listed in each description.
- **Drawn by code.** The doodles, black marker on white paper with pencil shading, are drawn and animated by the
  channel's own software. Pencil Buddy, the channel's mascot, draws things in as the story goes.
- **Checked by a person.** The narration is an AI voice. The channel owner watches every video before it is posted.
